pinky.

Pinky promise: put it down.

You and a friend agree how much Instagram is fine. Go over it, and they get a ping. Nothing gets blocked, and Pinky only sees which app is open, never what you do in it.

Pinky is in testing: free, no account, about five minutes to set up. Android and iPhone work together in one promise.

Android test onGoogle Play iPhone test onTestFlight

Android

  1. 1
    Join the testers group, signed in with the Google account your Play Store uses. Join the group
  2. 2
    Become a tester, then tap Download it on Google Play. Android test onGoogle Play
  3. 3
    Open Pinky and switch on what it asks for: it shows each step.

"App not available"? Wait 15 minutes after joining the group. "Item not found"? Switch the Play Store to the account you joined with.

iPhone

  1. 1
    Install TestFlight from the App Store: Apple's app for trying apps before they're out. Pinky needs iOS 16 or newer.
  2. 2
    Open the TestFlight link on your iPhone and tap Accept, then Install. iPhone test onTestFlight
  3. 3
    Open Pinky, allow Screen Time, pick Instagram and allow notifications.

Then make the promise

One of you taps Make a promise and sends the invite. The other taps the link and joins.
