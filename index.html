pinky.

Pinky promise: put it down.

Two of you agree how much Instagram is fine. Go over it, and the other one knows. Only which app is open is shared, never what you do in it.

Pinky is in testing. It's free, and Android and iPhone work together in one promise.

Android

  1. 1
    Join the testers group, signed in with the Google account your Play Store uses. Join the group
  2. 2
    Become a tester, then tap Download it on Google Play. Become a tester
  3. 3
    Open Pinky and switch on what it asks for: it shows each step.

"App not available"? Wait 15 minutes after joining the group. "Item not found"? Switch the Play Store to the account you joined with.

iPhone

  1. 1
    Install TestFlight from the App Store: Apple's app for trying apps before they're out.
  2. 2
    Open the invite on your iPhone and tap Accept, then Install. Get it on TestFlight
  3. 3
    Open Pinky, allow Screen Time, pick Instagram and allow notifications. iOS 16 or newer.

Then

One of you taps Make a promise and sends the invite; the other taps the link and joins. Something confusing or broken? Tell us: a screenshot helps.
