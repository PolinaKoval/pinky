What Pinky does

You and the people you choose make a promise on your phones: a daily allowance for the apps you pick and a sleep time. Pinky notices when a watched app is open on your own phone and tells the others in your promise when you go past the deal. Everything shared between phones is encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your promise code; we cannot read it. Our privacy policy says exactly what Pinky handles.

Using it fairly

  • Pinky is for adults keeping a promise with each other. It is not parental control and not for watching anyone who has not chosen to join. Everyone in a promise joined it with the code, and anyone can leave at any time.
  • Keep your promise code to the people in the promise. Anyone who has it can join and read the promise's messages.
  • Don't use Pinky to harass anyone, to send abusive nudges, or to try to break the relay or other people's promises.
  • You need to be 13 or older to use Pinky.
