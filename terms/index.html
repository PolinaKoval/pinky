What Pinky does

You and the people you choose make a promise on your phones: a daily allowance for the apps you pick and a sleep time. Pinky notices when a watched app is open on your own phone and tells the others in your promise when you go past the deal. Everything shared between phones is encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your promise code; we cannot read it. Our privacy policy says exactly what Pinky handles.

Using it fairly
