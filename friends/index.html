pinky.

For friends

Thanks for testing.

Pinky is a pinky promise to put your phone down. You and a friend agree how much Instagram a day is fine and when it's sleep time; go over, and the other one finds out. It's free and takes five minutes. Here is everything you need.

1. How to install

Android

  1. 1
    Join the testers group, signed in with the Google account your Play Store uses. If you were invited, the button says Accept invite; the "no permission" note under it is normal. Join the group
  2. 2
    Tap Become a tester (Google counts you only after this tap), then Download it on Google Play. Android test onGoogle Play

"App not available"? Wait 15 minutes after joining the group. "Item not found"? Switch the Play Store to the account you joined with. Had an old Pinky APK from us? Uninstall it first; Play can't install over it.

Android friends help most: Google needs 12 testers for 14 days before Pinky can go public. Please keep it installed, and the test joined, for two weeks: leaving early resets Google's count.

iPhone

  1. 1
    Install TestFlight from the App Store: Apple's app for trying apps before they're out. Pinky needs iOS 16 or newer.
  2. 2
    Open the link on your iPhone and tap Accept, then Install. iPhone test onTestFlight

Android and iPhone can be in the same promise.

2. How to start

  1. 1
    One of you opens Pinky and taps Make a promise. The other waits for the invite.
  2. 2
    Set the deal: turn the wheels for the minutes of Instagram a day that are fine and for sleep time. 0 minutes means every open counts.
  3. 3
    Tap Make the promise and send the invite link. Your friend taps it (or taps I have a code and pastes it), types a name and joins.
  4. 4
    Switch on what Pinky asks for: it shows each step. Android needs usage access and notifications; iPhone needs Screen Time, Instagram picked in Apple's list, and notifications. On Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo and similar phones, do the battery step too, or the phone puts Pinky to sleep at night.

Want more than two? Anyone in the promise can invite more friends from Edit on the promise card. Pinky Plus, the game, is sold through the stores and would cost you real money: ask us first, and we set you up so the test purchase is free.

3. What to try

Use it as you normally would, and try these on purpose at least once:

  • Get caught. Set a small allowance (or 0) and open Instagram for more than a minute. Your friend should hear about it. A look under a minute doesn't count.
  • Remind them. When your friend is caught, tap Pinky promise, …? on their block, or Write to … and send your own words.
  • Sleep time. Open Instagram after bedtime. You get a pink stop screen with Put it down and Open anyway. Put it down within a minute and it doesn't count.
  • Change the deal. Home, Edit on OUR PROMISE, turn a wheel, ask the others. They get a popup and tap Agree; everyone's phone should show the new deal.
  • Look away for a day. Leave the phone alone, then check the story on Home the next morning. Does it match what happened?
  • Be offline. Turn on flight mode, use Instagram, turn it off. The others should catch up.

4. How to report bugs

Anything confusing counts, not only crashes. If you had to guess what a button does, tell us. "All fine" is useful too: please write once around day 7 and once at the end.

  • Take a screenshot of what looked wrong, and say what you expected instead.
  • Add the Pinky version (Settings, at the bottom) and your phone model.
  • Android: Settings, Health, Copy, and paste it into your message. iPhone: a screenshot of Settings, Health.
  • Send it to Polina in any chat, or to reunite.studio.app@gmail.com. On iPhone, Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight attaches the details for you.
